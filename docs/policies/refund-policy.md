# Refund policy

**Shopify is the live source of truth** — Settings → Policies → Refund policy.
This file is the reviewable copy: edit here, then paste into Shopify so the two
never drift. The storefront's returns copy (`ORDER_TERMS`, `FAQS` and the
returns section of `/shipping-and-faq`) must agree with this text.

Two things in here are load-bearing and shouldn't be trimmed for brevity:

- **The withdrawal right is stated as covering made-to-order pieces.** The
  personalisation exemption in Directive 2011/83/EU covers goods made to a
  customer's own specification. A stock size in a stock colour is not that, so
  the exemption does not apply and "final sale" would not be enforceable.
- **Return postage being the customer's cost must be disclosed before purchase**
  for it to bind an EU consumer. That is why it also appears in `ORDER_TERMS`,
  which renders on the product page and in the cart — not only here.

Everything is made after you order it, so nothing here comes off a shelf. That
doesn't change your right to send it back.

## Keep what works, send back what doesn't

Every piece is sold and refunded individually, including pieces bought together
as a duo or a kit. You never have to return a whole order to return one item.

## 14 days to change your mind (EU & UK)

If you are in the EU or UK you have 14 days from delivery to withdraw from your
order, for any reason, under Directive 2011/83/EU and equivalent UK law. This
covers our made-to-order pieces: they are produced in standard sizes and stock
colours, not personalised to your specification, so the personalisation
exemption does not apply. Tell us within 14 days of delivery, then send the
items back within 14 days of telling us.

## 30 days everywhere else

Outside the EU and UK we give you 30 days from delivery on the same terms.

## Condition

Try a piece on the way you would in a shop. If it comes back beyond that — worn,
washed — we may reduce your refund to reflect the drop in value. That is the
standard the law sets and it is the one we use.

## Return postage

You cover return postage on a change-of-mind return. If an item is faulty,
damaged or not what you ordered, we cover it. Email us before sending anything
back and we will send the return address — parcels posted without it may not be
traceable.

## Refunds

Back to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the item, or
of you showing proof of postage, whichever comes first. If you return your whole
order we refund the original standard shipping too. If you return part of it, we
refund the items.

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items

Photograph it and email us within 30 days of delivery. We will replace it or
refund it in full, including postage both ways — your choice, not ours.
Separately, a statutory guarantee against lack of conformity applies for three
years under Spanish law, which governs these terms. Nothing on this page limits
that.

## Sizing

Every product page carries garment-flat measurements. Two minutes with a tape
measure against a garment you already own is the best way to never need this
page.

## Starting a return

Email hello@uniformish.store with your order number and which pieces are coming
back. We reply within two business days.
